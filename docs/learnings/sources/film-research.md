## 2. GEO competitors: shared ground, territory to avoid, white space

**What all four do:**
- **They open on a query or a problem line**, never on the product: a search bar (Ahrefs, Conductor), a provocation (Scrunch), a history montage (Profound).
- **They show engines as logos.** The ChatGPT knot, Gemini, the Perplexity mark and the G all appear as round icon badges or dropdown items (Ahrefs 0:11, Profound 0:28–0:30 and 0:53, Scrunch 0:11–0:13, Conductor 1:08).
- **They show brands as pills or orbs**: pill chips with a sparkle or logo (Ahrefs 0:03, Conductor 0:07.5) and floating logo orbs (Profound 1:09).
- **They prove it in a tilted dashboard.** Every one ends up in screen-recorded product UI with line or area charts, and three of the four do it in a dark theme.
- **Their scoreboards are generic**: donut "share of voice" (Ahrefs 0:27, Conductor 0:05), multi-line chart tabbed by engine (Ahrefs 0:30), stacked area (Conductor 0:30).

**Territory to avoid, because it already looks like them:**
- **A dark navy, blue or teal background with a glowing horizon.** Ahrefs and Conductor own it, and Robert has already rejected navy.
- **Orange used as a glow on dark.** Ahrefs's horizon is orange-on-blue, so our orange must sit on white and be solid, never a sunset gradient.
- **Warm off-white with flat orange-red shapes and black.** This is Scrunch's look (0:14–0:17). It sits closest to our "white + orange", so we need to differ by:
  - using pure cool white and a brighter, more saturated orange;
  - using light, glassy materials instead of Scrunch's dark-charcoal cards;
  - never using a big flat orange arch or slab.
- **A radar or sonar sweep** (Ahrefs), and **a donut or pie of share of voice** (Ahrefs, Conductor).
- **A wall of logo chips and a card-wall stagger** (Conductor).
- **Stock-footage and celebrity montage openers** (Profound).
- **"Brand vs brand through the ChatGPT knot"** is Profound's best moment (0:32.5). A plain three-in-a-row copy would read as theirs.

**What none of them do (white space for Acme):**
- **None shows an actual chat answer with the brand named inside the sentence.** All four abstract the answer into chips, bars or dashboards; Conductor's "Generate" rows show only lorem ipsum. The client likes chat bubbles, so this is open ground.
- **None compares the same question across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity side by side.** Engines only appear as tabs or a dropdown.
- **None shows rank as a physical position**, such as #1, #2 and #3 slots that brands move between. Profound's 1:06 "Industry Mention Volume" list is the closest, and it is static UI.
- **None uses one continuous camera move as its spine.** Ahrefs mostly glides, and the other three cut constantly.
- **None uses a light, bright, futuristic material**: frosted glass, light refraction, white volumetrics. Three are dark; Scrunch is matte and flat.
- **None treats tracking over time as a place you travel through.** Every one uses a 2D line chart.

## 3. Premium craft techniques (with numbers)

**Pacing.** The corpus median shot is **2.31 s** (54 videos; 90% interval 1.18–4.37 s). The median is **1.96 s** for feature reels
and **2.74 s** for narrated explainers. The coefficient of variation of shot length (`pace_cv`) has a median of **0.85**: shot lengths
vary on purpose. Our films span the range:
- Profound cuts every **1.36 s** (`pace_cv` 1.02).
- Conductor cuts every **3.49 s**.
- Scrunch **7.0 s**, Attio **6.7 s** and Linear **30 s, one shot** have long shots, yet `dead_hold_count` = **0** for all three.

**The lesson: a long take is premium only if something moves at every moment.** This is what Robert means by "no static frames
over 2–3 s". Two of the films break this rule: Profound has a 4.3 s dead hold (the 0:30 tweet card) and ClickUp a 6.2 s one.

Scene-length medians by type:
- kinetic statement card **3.23 s**
- comparison split **1.95 s**
- abstract shape metaphor **1.59 s**
- app icon orbit **0.99 s**
- chat demo panel **6.18 s** (the longest, so keep chat beats moving)
- logo close **6.86 s**

The median time to first UI is **6.4 s**. Cuts land on the beat **32%** of the time (median); Profound, Scrunch and Conductor sit at 0.38–0.40.

**Camera language:**
- **A continuous dolly through Z-space.** Scrunch flies through floating cards (0:05–0:10) and Linear tilts through 3D panels (0:11–0:15).
- **Push-through transitions.** The camera flies into a logo or shape that becomes the next frame: Profound's swoosh (0:38), ClickUp's ring (0:02.25), Attio's dot (0:07), Scrunch's arch (0:15). ClickUp's ring and Attio's dot are also matched shapes.
- `ui_camera_push_in` has a median of **562 ms** and is ease-out **43%** of the time (n=40).
- `whip_transition` has a median of **1.40 s** (ClickUp 0:27).

**Type:**
- One short line, centred and small, over a large field: Attio, Scrunch, Notion.
- Words appear by type-on or swap one at a time ("Every email / call / note").
- One big kinetic word where it counts: Profound's "BIG" (0:13).
- Mono uppercase with a block cursor reads as tech (Linear 0:22).
- `kinetic_word_reveal` has a median of **577 ms**, and the word stagger is **84 ms** (corpus).

**Transitions:** the push-through and match cut (`match_cut_element`, **224 ms**), blur crossfade (**442 ms**), chip append (**281 ms**) and
layout reframe (**624 ms**, the most common: n=106).

**Depth and materials:**
- Tilted UI planes with shallow depth of field: Linear 0:12, Profound 0:52.
- Spheres with soft shadows: Scrunch 0:32.
- Glowing outlines on a selected control: Profound's "All models" yellow glow at 0:53.
- Frosted, light glass appears in none of these films; it is open territory.

**Colour:**
- Each film commits to a 2–3-colour world and uses colour as punctuation.
- Notion and Scrunch flash a full-frame colour field for about 1 s (Notion blue 0:01–0:02, Scrunch orange 0:15–0:17).
- ClickUp switches wallpaper colour at each section change.

**Motion tokens (corpus):**
- Ease-out (`circ.out`) **35%**, ease-in-out **23%**, spring overshoot **14%**.
- 63% of element groups move all at once rather than staggered.
- `chat_bubble_append` has a median of **589 ms** and is ease-out 90% of the time.
- `ai_answer_stream` lands one piece every **238 ms** (median).

**Sound:** the corpus median is **38 SFX/min**. Conductor has 39.8 and ClickUp 80. Attio (5.1), Notion (1.1) and Linear (0) are almost music-only,
and they are the "premium-calm" end.

## 4. Eight idea seeds (no city)

1. **Answer-bubble cold open.** White frame. A chat bubble types "What's the best CRM for a 10-person team?", the answer streams in, and three brand
   names light up orange inside the sentence. Then the camera pulls back to reveal the same question asked of three engines.
   *Builds on* Ahrefs `ahrefs-…mp4` 0:00–0:05 (query + brand pills). We swap the pills for a real sentence, which is the white space in §2.
2. **Three engines, one question: a triptych of bubbles.** ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answer side by side, and the client's brand is named in one
   answer, missing in another and ranked #3 in the third. *Builds on* Notion `notion-…mp4` 0:14.75–0:16.75 ("Scanning Slack / Drive" chip cycle) and Profound 0:53 (models dropdown).
3. **Fly into the brand name.** The camera pushes into the orange brand name inside a bubble, and the word becomes the next scene's whole frame
   (a white, frosted rank board). *Builds on* Profound `profound-…mp4` 0:37–0:38.75 (swoosh push-through) and ClickUp 0:02.25 (ring zoom).
4. **Rank as a physical place.** In one continuous Z-dolly, the camera passes floating glass slots #1–#5, and brand chips slide between them as the
   engines "vote". Ours ends with the client moving up. *Builds on* Scrunch `scrunch-…mp4` 0:05.5–0:10 (3D card flight) and Linear 0:11–0:15 (tilted panels in depth).
5. **Head-to-head through the engine.** The client sits left, the competitor right, and the Acme orb sits in the centre, fed by thin lines from three engine
   icons. Orange tick marks fill under each brand. To differ from Profound, use white glass and our orb rather than a borrowed logo.
   *Builds on* Profound 0:32.5–0:36.5 (Nike / ChatGPT / lululemon score dashes).
6. **Time as a ruler you travel along.** A white horizontal timeline glides past ("Week 1 · Week 2 · …"). On each tick, a small answer bubble pops up
   naming or omitting the brand, and an orange line threads the hits. This is tracking over time without a dashboard.
   *Builds on* Attio `attio-…mp4` 0:24–0:28.25 (ruler with data labels).
7. **Mentions as converging threads.** Fine orange threads stream from many prompts and merge into the brand's line. The more mentions, the brighter
   it burns; competitor threads stay grey. A white-world version of Linear. *Builds on* Linear `linear-…mp4` 0:00–0:09 (branching threads + mono IDs).
8. **Slot-machine question chip.** A big line, "When people ask AI for [best running shoes / a CRM / a dentist near me]…", cycles the chip every 0.3 s,
   then lands on "…does it say your name?". The orange cursor block becomes the Acme mark. *Builds on* Notion 0:04.5–0:07.75
   ("One AI tool to [chip]"), Scrunch 0:00.5–0:04 (provocation type-on) and Linear 0:22–0:27 (block cursor).

Seeds 1→3→4→6 chain into one continuous camera flight, the "one take" Robert liked:
bubble → push into the name → rank slots → timeline → logo.
