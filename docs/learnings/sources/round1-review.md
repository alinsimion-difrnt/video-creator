## i01 White One Take: 8 · 7 · 8 · 7 · 8 · 8 = **46**

**Best**
1. **0.0–3.9 s: the strongest opening of the set.** A deep white city with real planar shadows, haze and a lit
   horizon, chat bubbles everywhere, and the camera already flying. The white has depth: objects are a step brighter
   than the floor. That is exactly "d1 one take, but white".
2. **17.9–20.3 s: the empty 5th slot → logo.** A dashed orange "Your brand?" row glows on the glass card, the camera
   pushes through it, and orange particles assemble Acme on "Ack-me" (20.2 s). Then a fly-through of the O
   (21.6–22.2 s). The best cause-and-effect chain in the whole review.
3. **3.9–9.6 s: the typing ride.** Each question types in sync in a large bubble (about 40 px at 4.6 s), with the
   bubble flown past rather than faded out. Real one-take grammar.

**Worst**
1. **11.0–16.0 s: the ChatGPT beat and the "short list" beat are weak.** On "ChatGPT" (11.58 s) we get a small
   logo in a pale orbit diagram. Then at 14.3–16.0 s the answer card is a blurred, empty glass slab while the voice
   says "short list". That is a placeholder state, and it lands on the line that matters most.
2. **22.3–24.3 s: thin void.** After the O fly-through it is white space with a few orange dots and grey spheres
   ("tracks which brands AI recommends" has no image).
3. **27.2–30.9 s: hand-off and ending.** The day gates flash past in 0.8 s (WED–FRI unreadable), and the end frame
   is small, centred and static-feeling (rival labels about 30 px, a stray orange line from the bottom edge).

**Survive with the client? YES.** It is literally his favourite idea, in his colours, and it opens like a premium
film. Fix the ChatGPT beat and the 22–28 s stretch and it is the frontrunner.

## i02 Question Swarm: 6 · 6 · 6 · 7 · 5 · 5 = **35**

**Best**
1. **20.1–21.0 s: the swarm becomes the logo.** Hundreds of bubbles turn orange and lock into the real Acme
   geometry, then the vector mark resolves over them. A genuinely earned signature move.
2. **4.0–9.6 s: the question stack.** Three white bubbles type top-left, stacking into a readable list (about
   34 px), while the cloud swirls behind. Clear and on the words.
3. **28.8–30.9 s: rivals as rings of bubbles**, with Acme's ring orange and a "Thu · daily scan" chip. Readable
   on a phone.

**Worst**
1. **0.0–3.0 s: grey carpet.** 400 small identical bubbles over a pale grey-blue city; no hero, no focal point,
   everything at the same value. The whole film sits in a cold #E8ECF1 band: it is grey, not white.
2. **14.4–19.5 s: the short list looks like a wireframe.** The bubbles that become "bars" are slate-blue pills,
   so the answer card reads as skeleton loading bars, and they fly across "Northwind" while it lands (14.8–15.3 s).
3. **21.8–27.6 s: the engines beat is a shapeless blob.** The swarm wanders over the city with three tiny engine
   icons (about 30 px) at the top. On a phone "ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity" is invisible.

**Survive? NO.** One clever moment (the logo), but the overall image is grey, busy and low-contrast, and the
product story collapses after 21 s.

## i03 The Prism: 5 · 8 · 6 · 6 · 8 · 6 = **39**

**Best**
1. **16.8–19.5 s: the most ownable single image of the ten.** ChatGPT's glass prism splits the beam into four
   named spectral bands; the warm end of the spectrum is missing and drawn as an orange dashed "Your brand?" band.
   "You are the missing colour" is a brilliant, brand-true metaphor, and the refraction/dispersion is real
   (WebGL FBO), so it looks expensive.
2. **12.0–15.0 s: the question becomes light.** The CRM bubble collapses into a pulse, enters the prism, and on
   "short list" the beam fans into a spectrum. Physical cause and effect.
3. **28.4–30.9 s: the three spectra bend into one ranked bar chart** and Acme's dashed band becomes a solid
   orange bar on "pick". A real payoff, not a new chart cut in.

**Worst**
1. **0.0–2.5 s: a small pale prism at the right edge with a fan of white rods.** Nearly static for two seconds,
   white on white. Fails the "striking by 0.3 s" rule.
2. **No city, and the palette adds violet/blue/green.** The bubbles over the city he likes are gone (pills on
   rods instead), and the spectrum brings three new hues into a white + orange brand. Clever, but off-brief.
3. **21.4–24.4 s: three empty pale prisms** wait for their words (placeholder state), the 23.3 s glint is
   invisible, and the engine logos etched in the glass are small (about 30 px).

**Survive? MAYBE as a moment, not as the film.** The "missing colour" frame is worth stealing for one beat;
30 s (let alone 105 s) of rods and prisms on grey-white is too delicate and too far from his city.

### 3. Recurring failures across builders
1. **Placeholder states on the most important lines.** Empty or skeleton answer cards right on "ChatGPT" or
   "short list": i01 14.3–16.0, i09 11.4–13.6, i10 10.6–14.4, i02 14.4. Empty machines or prisms waiting for
   their words: i07 22.0–24.4, i03 22.4–24.4. The brief already banned this; eight builders still did it.
2. **"It tracks which brands AI recommends" (21.8–24.4 s) has no image in 8 of 10.** Builders spent everything
   on the logo and then coasted: voids (i01), blobs (i02), flat diagrams (i04), hold shots (i07, i10).
3. **The logo lands late.** "Ack-me" is spoken at 20.24 s; i04 completes at about 21.7, i07 at 21.25, i08 at about
   21.0. Only i01, i05, i06 and i09 land on the word.
4. **"Every day" gets under a second** (i01 0.8 s gates, i05 under 1 s, i07 small sheets). It is the core product
   promise and deserves a 1.5–2 s time-lapse.
5. **Small text.** Labels of 18–30 px in i08 (planet chips), i06 (HUD, engine stations), i04, i01's ending and
   i03's engine names. Everything meaningful under 32 px dies on a phone.
6. **White-on-white drifting into grey-blue.** i02, i06, i09, i04 and i10 sit in a cold #E6EBF1–#C9D6E6 band,
   which reads grey/clinical, not "white and orange". The ones that look white (i01, i08) do it with value steps,
   shadows and a warm glow, not with tint.
7. **Weak first 0.3 s.** i03, i04 and i05 open small and pale. The lesson was already written; only i01, i08, i10 and
   (partly) i06 obeyed it.
8. **Endings that are small and static**, with too much empty frame (i01, i04, i08). Several also imply that Acme
   ends up #1 (i07, i10 in Gemini), a claim the product does not make.
9. **Inconsistent naming of the empty 5th slot:** "Your brand?", "Yours?", "Acme?", "Acme CRM?". Decide one rule:
   "Your brand?" on the empty slot, "Acme" only after the logo, when we show example data.
10. **No sound design at all.** Every fly-through, lock, scan and tick is silent; half of "premium" is missing.

### 4. What we now know about building this in HyperFrames (synthesis of all LEARNINGS.md)

**The architecture that works (all ten converged on it)**
- **One `<canvas>` redrawn purely from t.** One linear GSAP proxy tween drives `apply(t)` and everything (camera,
  geometry, overlays, typed text) is derived from t. This avoids the CSS-3D layer limit and is seek-safe by
  construction. Keep the engine in `assets/engine.js` and only the `window.__timelines` registration inline
  (lint fails otherwise: `missing_timeline_registry`, `composition_file_too_large`).
- **`src/build.py` inlines real SVG paths** (logos as `Path2D`, Gemini gradients rebuilt) into the built comp.
- **Camera: monotone cubic (Fritsch–Carlson) keys per channel.** Catmull-Rom/naive Hermite overshoots and makes
  the camera reverse (i01, i04). i08's orbit rig (target, dist, pitch, yaw) has no singularity at top-down and was
  reused unchanged by i10. Check that z-speed never goes negative (i01's `review/cam.js`).
- **HTML for all text, positioned with the same projection as the canvas.** Crisp at any scale. Clamp scale
  (i04: 0.95–1.2) so text never drops under 30 px. HTML always paints over the canvas, so anything passing "in
  front" must fade the text first (i07). CSS-3D `matrix3d` driven by the same look-at basis also matches exactly
  (i09) and keeps text crisp.
- **Precompute anything iterative, then play it back by t.** Boids baked with numpy to a 15 fps table and
  Catmull-Rom interpolated (i02); sweep angle and orbit clocks as integrated 480 Hz tables with `invTheta` to find
  the exact time a beam crosses a bearing (i06, i08); closed-form particle phases with curl noise that is zero at
  rest (i05); Kepler orbits solved analytically (i08); closed-form shatter physics (i10); 2-link IK arms (i07).
- **Place story objects where the motion will be on the word** (i06: bubbles at the beam's bearing at word time),
  instead of animating objects to the word.

**White-on-white that reads**
- Value steps, not tint: floor #DCE2E9 → #EEF1F5, objects #FFFFFF, faces in cool greys; the hero object is
  always the brightest thing (i01, i03).
- Planar projected shadows in two quarter-res blurred layers (contact and soft), navy-tinted at low alpha (i01).
  Ellipse blob shadows look like puddles.
- A 7.5 % navy hairline on every face/edge makes white geometry readable (i07, i10 shards).
- Frosted glass = a downsampled copy of the frame (480x270 + 3 px blur) clipped to the panel, `evenodd` for holes
  (i01). Glass on white reads through its edges: outline, bevel, fresnel rim, one specular streak (i03).
- Orange light pools on the floor sell "light" on white (i01).

**Gotchas worth a checklist**
- Never `ctx.filter = blur()` at full res per frame; blur at quarter res and upscale (i07: 6 min → 2 min).
- WebGL renders through SwiftShader (software). No per-pixel loops; rasterise quads into an FBO and sample it
  (i03). Benchmark with i03's `bench/run.sh` before any full render.
- Reset per-frame state on every branch; a stale variable is a seek-safety bug (i04's frozen dots). Never feed
  one frame's positions into the next (i09).
- Near-plane clip every edge once the camera flies through things (i04). Fade text the camera flies through under
  ~900 units (i01).
- `check`: use `visibility:hidden` for untyped text, never `color:transparent` (`text_not_painted`, i07); declare
  `data-layout-allow-occlusion`/`-overlap`/`-overflow` on the text element itself (i06, i09, i10).
- `snapshot` calls Gemini if `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set: always `env -u GEMINI_API_KEY ... --describe false` (i06).
- Renders started with `&` inside a tool call die with `render_cancelled_parent_exited`; use background mode (i03).
- Canvas text needs fonts loaded: rebuild textures when `document.fonts.status === "loaded"` (i10).
- freezedetect is stricter on final "looks" renders than drafts (i05). Any hold over 1 s needs drift.
- Review at 640 px or bigger; 384 px strips are useless for white-on-white (i02). Use 10 fps strips for every move.
- Wide contrails on white read as needles (i08); white bubbles vanish into white cards, switch them to ink when
  they lock (i02); blurred orange sprites read as stains (i04).

**What we do not know well yet, and how to learn it**
1. **Real 3D materials on white (AO, bevels, soft area light, glossy floor).** i07 and i04 both say their 2D fakes
   stall at about 70 %. Learn: vendor three.js locally, benchmark a 300-box city with `MeshStandardMaterial` +
   baked AO under SwiftShader using i03's bench harness; target ≤0.15 s/frame. Fallback: bake the city's AO and
   shadows to a texture once, then only move the camera.
2. **Motion blur and depth of field.** Nobody has them; fast moves (i01's dives, i10's shatter) strobe. Learn:
   sub-frame accumulation is free to try because everything is a pure function of t (draw t−δ…t+δ at low alpha,
   or render 2–4 sub-samples into an offscreen canvas). Measure the cost against i10's 60 s render. DOF: i04's
   bokeh sprite pyramid and i05's circle-of-confusion splats already work; generalise them.
3. **Sound design synced to events.** Zero builders did it. Learn: export each comp's event list (word times plus
   our own beats: locks, scans, passes) to JSON and assemble SFX with a local mixer (ffmpeg `adelay`/`amix`) from a
   licensed library; no paid APIs.
4. **GPU particles** (i05 wants 200k+ points): the closed-form phases ported to a vertex shader, phases as
   textures. Test throughput under SwiftShader first; it may not beat CPU splatting.
5. **Path planning that avoids text** (i02's bars flying across names, i10's shards across pills): cost-based
   routing or Hungarian assignment with label boxes as obstacles.
6. **A shared kit.** Ten builders rewrote the same camera, projection, `apply(t)` loop, SVG inliner and typing
   helper. Extract one `world-kit` (camera rig from i08, shadows and glass from i01, sweep/clock integrals from i06,
   textured triangles from i10, FBO glass from i03) before the full film, with a small test page per module.

### 5. Recommendation for the full film

**Direction: i01's white one-take world as the spine, with i08's orbits for the AI answer, i07's scan for

white city, bubbles everywhere, orange only for "you".

**The 5 most important improvements to make it premium**
1. **No placeholder ever, and an image for every line.** Answer text streams in the moment ChatGPT appears;
   engines light with content on their word; "tracks which brands" gets the scan; "every day" gets a 1.5–2 s
   time-lapse. Logo complete by 20.3 s. Add a CI-style check: no card/panel visible >0.3 s without content.
2. **A warm-white material system with real depth.** i01's value steps, planar shadows, haze and frosted glass,
   plus i08's pearl/chip finish; AO and bevel highlights (baked or three.js); a warm key light with orange light
   that actually spills onto the floor. Ban the steel-blue tints (#C9D6E6 family) outright.
3. **Phone-first typography and composition.** Questions ≥44 px, meaningful labels ≥32 px, HUD purely decorative;
   max three moving labels; the hero object fills ≥40 % of frame; no frame more than 30 % empty for more than 0.5 s.
4. **Camera language and motion finish.** One continuous camera with three verbs (dive, orbit, pull back),
   sub-frame motion blur on fast moves, shallow DOF to separate glass layers on white, and a striking 0.3 s (i01's
   city with a hero bubble rising into the lens).
5. **Sound design locked to events.** Whooshes on dives and the O fly-through, a soft lock tick per reticle, a
   chime when the orange slot lights, a laser hum on the scan, a tick per day on the time-lapse. Exported from the
   same event list as the visuals, mixed locally.
