# Pixel-art study: findings (track 2)

Written 2026-10-13 from every note in `studies/pixel-art/notes/` (125 notes: 68 subjects + 57 TikTok clips),
the six GIF metrics files in `studies/pixel-art/metrics/` (exact frame timings, code-measured) and the 57 clip
measures in `studies/pixel-art/clips/` (rough: TikTok copies, compression and camera moves add changes).

Rule for this page: every number names its file. Numbers in the notes that came from articles are left out or
described in words.

Why this study: Robert's note on the last Fred & Pixie reel was that the animations were overused and that Fred
never acted *in and out* of the scene (exit, come back, do it again, go away, act small in the background).

Short answer from the study: **fewer, held poses; one effect per beat; and use the frame edge and the depth of
the scene as acting tools.**

Rules this implies:
- **Base hold is 80-100 ms per drawing** (10-12.5 drawings/s). 29 of the 39 GIFs have a median hold of 80 or
  100 ms. Body moves (run, jump, slide, wall slide, squash, easing) all sit at 80 ms; walks and the idle at
  100 ms (`saint11-movement.json`, `saint11-timing.json`, `saint11-acting.json`).
- **The hold is uniform; emphasis comes from rare exceptions**, not from smooth in-betweens. Almost every GIF uses
  one delay, and the exceptions are the point of the lesson.
- **A blink is one short frame inside slower holds.** Cuteness.gif uses 30 ms against a 120 ms median
  (`saint11-acting.json`, delays 30/60/100/120).
- **An impact is one very short frame.** Impact-export.gif has one 20 ms frame inside 80 ms holds
  (`saint11-timing.json`).
- **The wind-up is held.** AttackSheet.gif uses a 300 ms delay against 100 ms holds, with merged holds up to
  400 ms. The break/death beat ends on a 400 ms settle (Breaking.gif) (`saint11-timing.json`,
  `saint11-acting.json`).
- **An idle is long and calm.** characterIdle.gif is 60 drawings over a 6000 ms cycle at 100 ms
  (`saint11-acting.json`). A breathing loop that repeats every second reads as busy.
- **Effects are the fastest and shortest thing on screen.** Wind and glitch run at 60 ms (16.67/s). The whole
  glitch loop is 840 ms, electric 1230 ms, explosion 1600 ms (`saint11-effects.json`).
- **Scenes rest for seconds.** City.gif holds 1500 and 3000 ms; Silhouette.gif opens on 1800 ms; Shine.gif sweeps
  at 80 ms then rests 200-700 ms between glints (`saint11-scenes.json`, `saint11-form.json`).

## 3. Acting in and out of the scene

This is what our last reel lacked. Each pattern below comes with subjects that show it.

- **Enter with a real move, not a slide.**
  - Crouch, launch, land with a squash; or arrive mid-move so the shot starts with energy (saint11-movement,
    celeste).
  - Outline draws on, fills, then the sprite appears (sea-of-stars).
  - Turn-to-face reveal or flash burst (pokemon-black-white).
  - Hide behind foreground cover and pop out (clip 7122902315601792262).
  - Peek in from the frame edge (clip 7684489268080758023).
- **Exit as an event.**
  - Knocked off-frame after a white flash (saint11-acting).
  - Flash plus a small puff (sly-8-bit, saint11-effects).
  - Dissolve into pixels (loop-hero, hyper-light-drifter).
  - Transform away (castlevania-sotn).
  - An edge-on flip that vanishes (clip 7661081606890753301).
  - Afterimage streaks on a dash (clip 7516969157049519383).
- **Leave something behind.** The empty frame still tells the story:
  - bodies and splats stay (hotline-miami);
  - debris settles (noita);
  - shards stay on the floor (saint11-acting).

  The held empty beat before the return is part of the gag.
- **Come back with one change.**
  - Same home shot, new progress each life (minit).
  - Enter, look, react, leave, next (moonlighter).
  - A running gag that escalates, battered but back (mother-3, metal-slug).
  - Pops up later in an unexpected spot (undertale).
  - Approach, refused, back off, try again; the third try differs (nes-limits).
- **Repeat with variation, never identical.** Each repeat changes one thing:
  - the reaction gets worse (karadio_ 7565439932848672031);
  - the object differs, rule of three with a twist (siegfriedcroes 7662403835691846945);
  - the pose is new on each exit (metal-slug).

  Identical loops are what read as "overused".
- **Act small in the background.**
  - A tiny figure on a path or strip while the main content runs (loop-hero, the papers-please queue strip).
  - Tiny in a sharp tilt-shift band with a blurred foreground (hd-2d, octopath-traveler).
  - On a pale far parallax layer, like the car crossing the street in saint11-scenes.
  - Doing chores in the background (eastward).

  At that size, keep only the identity cues and act with 1-2 px shifts (sly-tiny).
- **Swap near and far.** The same character at two scales:
  - a strip and a booth (papers-please);
  - sprite small, portrait cut-in for the reaction (stardew-valley, chained-echoes);
  - near from behind, far facing us (pokemon-black-white);
  - a tiny silhouette, then a hard cut to an extreme close-up (dead-cells);
  - a tiny sprite, then a cut to a giant chunky face (matiarms 7560057671085215032).
- **React instead of act.**
  - The big thing moves and the hero holds: a colossus behind a still hero (hyper-light-drifter, animal-well,
    terraria).
  - A held pose while the world changes (ryusaque 7659546169646861575, blasphemous).
  - The cause small in the back, the reaction big in front (karadio_).
  - Pause, then react (mother-3, undertale).
- **Two characters: one acts, one freezes.**
  - Theatre staging (final-fantasy-6).
  - A familiar acting on its own beat (castlevania-sotn).
  - One works while the other leans and sways on a different beat (pixosly loops).
  - Walking closer as escalation (chained-echoes).

## 4. Palette, light, silhouette and texture

- **Colour count: small and fixed.**
  - saint11 sheets measure a median of 17 colours, from 4 (1-bit.gif) to 31 (Glitch.gif), UI included.
  - Character sheets use 16-19 (`saint11-acting.json`), movement sheets 11-20 (`saint11-movement.json`).
  - Fix one palette before drawing (lospec-tutorials, pixeljoint-tutorials, sly-style).
  - Retro looks go much lower: downwell and minit use 2-3 colours by eye, game-boy 4.
- **Hue-shifted ramps.**
  - Across the saint11 GIFs, warm ramps (4+ colours) shift by a median of about +33°, cool ones by about −17°
    (all six `metrics/saint11-*.json`).
  - Skin and fur in `saint11-form.json` shift +51° to +64°.
  - Lights drift warm/yellow, darks cool/purple (sly-palettes, owlboy, sea-of-stars).
  - Many hit TikTok scenes are strongly shifted too (rough: pixosly 7610485362309254407 has a median of 81.7° in
    `clips/`).
- **Shadow temperature.**
  - Most subjects put cool shadows (blue, purple, teal) under warm light: owlboy, katana-zero, sea-of-stars,
    kingdom-two-crowns, the lofi clips.
  - Cozy interiors flip it to warm brown shadows: stardew-valley, chained-echoes, metal-slug.
  - Never plain grey-black.
- **One light, fixed.**
  - One key light from a top corner, with a cast or contact shadow under the character (sly-light,
    derek-yu-tutorial, saint11-form, mother-3's oval shadow, street-fighter-3's ellipse).
  - Keep it the same across the reel, so a smaller, softer shadow reads as distance (sly-light).
- **Outlines.**
  - Selective: dark only on the shadow side and under the body, light or broken where the light hits, coloured
    rather than black (derek-yu-tutorial, saint11-form, huyh1230's pink outlines).
  - Never anti-alias the outer edge.
  - Use a full dark outline when the background is busy (nes-limits, terraria, metal-slug).
- **Hero vs background contrast.**
  - A low-contrast, desaturated background behind a high-contrast, saturated hero with one accent colour
    (blasphemous red, downwell red, papers-please's one accent per face, sly-palettes' neutral row,
    saint11-form).
  - One warm hero on a cool world reads at a few pixels (animal-well, owlboy, hyper-light-drifter).
- **Silhouette first.**
  - A pose must read as a flat grey shape. Test it desaturated and at thumbnail size (saint11-form,
    derek-yu-tutorial, the game-boy 4-value test, pixeljoint-tutorials).
  - Big head and a big identity shape such as hair, a hat or a horn (celeste, stardew-valley, shovel-knight,
    mother-3).
- **Texture by clusters, not noise.** Dither covers only 0.5-2.7% of pixels across all saint11 GIFs (median
  1.4%; the 2.7% is 1-bit.gif) in all six metrics files. Texture comes from clusters and particles.
- **Scale cleanly.**
  - Integer, nearest-neighbour scale only, and one sprite scale per shot (sly-basics, pixeljoint-tutorials).
  - Filtered 3D and AI "pixel" images lack a clean grid and read soft (clips 7235359988707953926,
    7507648967178554632, 7427568589772754207).

## 5. Effects and restraint

When effects help:
- **To mark an event**: an entrance, an exit, a hit, a reveal.
  - A flash before, particles that dissipate after (saint11-effects).
  - One flash frame and a short trail on the one big hit (dead-cells).
  - One smear frame, then a still hold (katana-zero, sly-melee, saint11-timing).
- **When they are short.** The measured effects last 0.8-1.6 s for glitch, electric and explosion
  (`saint11-effects.json`). Their power is the contrast with calm.
- **When the effect does the acting for a still hero** (octopath-traveler, hd-2d, noita, hyper-light-drifter).

When they clutter:
- **Everything moving at once.** The calm subjects animate one thing:
  - cinemagraph, mark-ferrari, waneella, colour-cycling;
  - one TV screen (pixeljess 7170936314408324398, rough median hold 400 ms in `clips/`);
  - one traffic light (sixveeceear 7288486867673107719).
- **The same effect on every exit** (the saint11-effects note).
- **The same move everywhere.** xhabitsss 7649108849101737230 slides and mirrors one pose for a whole clip,
  which is exactly our "overused" look.
- **Constant bounce.** pokemon-black-white and sly-idle keep the idle quiet and save the big moves. When Fred
  only listens, move only his eyes and mouth (bean0ill887 7527151951826455840, boosty_bear 7694913885018623239).
- **Bloom, shake or freeze-time everywhere.** hd-2d and sly-melee both say: one key moment per piece.

Rule of thumb from this study: **per beat, one moving thing; per reel, one big effect moment; every other
second is a held pose or ambient life.** The stillest clips are not the weakest: ryusaque 7659546169646861575
measures a 5800 ms median hold (`clips/`, rough) and is pure emotion.

## 6. Style atlas

- **Cozy**: warm wood interiors with warm-brown shadows, or a cool night room with one warm lamp on the
  characters; small loops at calm holds. stardew-valley, chained-echoes, moonlighter, pixosly clips, waneella,
  eastward.
- **Dramatic**: one-hue scene tint, a tiny still hero against a giant, a backlight disc or blackout plus flash,
  one red accent. hyper-light-drifter, dead-cells, blasphemous, kirokaze, animal-well.
- **Comic**: big heads, held reaction poses, pause-then-react, running gags, one pose swap per joke. metal-slug,
  mother-3, undertale, nes-limits, karadio_, siegfriedcroes.
- **Retro**: 2-4 colours or NES limits, few frames held long, a text box with a tiny portrait. game-boy,
  nes-limits, one-bit, downwell, minit, shovel-knight.
- **Techy**: neon one-hue rooms, a glitch or hologram (blue ramp, scanlines, row offsets) for 1-2 frames, an
  RGB-split smear, a VHS rewind as a "try again". katana-zero, saint11-effects (Glitch, Holograms), hotline-miami,
  isometric for product-feature build-ups.
- **Mood/lofi breather**: a tiny figure in a big night set with one warm light, held 1-3 s. vorty24, pixei_021,
  k1rby_jka1, idk.ery, sixveeceear clips.

## 7. For Fred and Pixie: rules for the next reel

1. **Build the reel on exits and returns.** Fred leaves the frame at least twice and comes back each time with
   one visible change (a new pose, a new prop, a worse reaction). Never the same entrance twice. (minit,
   moonlighter, metal-slug, undertale)
2. **Give Fred a home shot.** Use the same framing on every return so the change reads instantly. (minit,
   nes-limits)
3. **Make each exit an event, and a different one each time:**
   - knocked off by a flash;
   - a flash plus a puff;
   - a dissolve into pixels;
   - an edge-on flip;
   - a dash with 2-3 fading afterimages.

   (saint11-acting, sly-8-bit, loop-hero, clip 7661081606890753301, clip 7516969157049519383)
4. **Hold the empty frame a beat after he leaves**, and leave evidence (a splash, a knocked prop) so the empty
   scene still tells the joke. (hotline-miami, noita, saint11-acting)
5. **One "small in the background" beat per reel.** Fred is tiny on a far, paler parallax layer or in a sharp
   tilt-shift band, doing one small task with 1-2 px moves. Then cut to him big and close. (saint11-scenes,
   hd-2d, sly-tiny, papers-please, loop-hero)
6. **Swap near and far between Fred and Pixie.** One acts close while the other does a chore small in the back,
   then they trade. The frozen one stays still. (eastward, final-fantasy-6, pokemon-black-white)
7. **A breathing idle, quiet and long.**
   - A 1-2 px bob with a blink on its own beat, at 100 ms holds over a long cycle (characterIdle.gif: 6000 ms,
     `saint11-acting.json`).
   - The blink is one short frame (Cuteness.gif's 30 ms).
   - Use it only while he waits. (saint11-acting, sly-idle, pokemon-black-white)
8. **When Fred listens, move only his eyes and mouth**, with poses held 133-150 ms (rough, `clips/` for
   7527151951826455840 and 7694913885018623239).
9. **A big move is hold, snap, hold**, once or twice per reel on the key word:
   - a held wind-up (AttackSheet.gif's 300 ms delay);
   - one smear frame;
   - a very short impact frame (Impact-export.gif's 20 ms);
   - a settle.

   (saint11-timing, sly-melee, katana-zero)
10. **Body moves at 80 ms, everything else at 100 ms; no smooth tweening of the sprite across the frame.** He
    swims in with a crouch, launch and squash. (`saint11-movement.json`; saint11-movement, celeste)
11. **One effect moment per reel, one moving thing per beat.**
    - Effects are short (0.8-1.6 s in `saint11-effects.json`) and dissipate.
    - The rest of the scene is a cinemagraph with one ambient loop, such as a water colour-cycle or a lamp.

    (saint11-effects, cinemagraph, colour-cycling, mark-ferrari)
12. **Repeat gags in threes, with escalation.** Same setup, the reaction a bit worse each time, and the third
    breaks the pattern. The cause is tiny in the back, Fred's reaction big in front. (karadio_
    7565439932848672031, siegfriedcroes, nes-limits, mother-3)
13. **A pose library, not loops.** Nod, head-shake, surprise hop, smug hold, back turned; cut between them on the
    narration beats. Hold the sad or smug pose for a full beat. (chrono-trigger, final-fantasy-6, shovel-knight,
    sly-castlevania)
14. **The look:**
    - one fixed palette of about 16-20 colours (saint11 character sheets use 16-19, `saint11-acting.json`);
    - hue-shifted ramps: warm lights, cool or purple shadows;
    - a selective dark-orange outline;
    - a contact shadow and one key light;
    - integer scale only;
    - Fred is the warm, saturated thing on a desaturated, cooler background.

    (sly-palettes, owlboy, derek-yu-tutorial, sly-light, sly-basics)
15. **Test before shipping.** View Fred and Pixie as flat grey silhouettes and at phone-thumbnail size. If a pose
    does not read, change the shape, not the colour. (saint11-form, game-boy, pixeljoint-tutorials)

## 8. Limits of this study

- **The exact timings come from one artist.**
  - All 39 measured GIFs are saint11 tutorial sheets: illustrative loops, not shipped game sprites.
  - Their colour counts include tutorial text and panels.
  - The code's squash/stretch measure reads 1.0 on almost every GIF, so it did not detect squash. The squash
    claims come from frames and notes.
- **Games were seen through TikTok.**
  - Game and artist notes use up to 3 most-liked TikToks under the tag, plus wiki text.
  - Many clips were off-subject (cosplay, anime edits, dance videos, a band, a namesake).
  - So celeste, castlevania-sotn, chrono-trigger, final-fantasy-6, sword-and-sworcery, eboy, paul-robertson and
    slynyrd rest mostly or wholly on text. The paul-robertson note says to get real frames before copying his
    timing.
- **The TikTok tag clips were often not pixel art** (19 of 57) or not hand-placed (4 more), and almost none were
  gameplay with characters entering and exiting. The acting patterns in section 3 come mostly from games and
  tutorials, not from what performs on TikTok.
- **TikTok copies blur the pixel grid.**
  - Re-encoding and phone-filmed screens inflate change counts: many clips read as about 30 changes/s from camera
    motion.
  - The end card breaks palette counts: several clips read a palette_size of 1.
  - Clip numbers in `clips/` are rough and used only as cross-checks.
- **YouTube is not yet fetched.** Robert's 10 creators and the Kyle Bunk GDC talk are missing, so long-form
  animation tutorials (MortMort, AdamCYounis, Pixel Pete) are not in these rules yet.
- **Articles were paraphrased.** Figures from articles (frame counts, ms) were not measured and are left out of
  the numbered rules.
- **Likes are not causation.** The most-liked clips are memes and craft videos; we did not test which pixel
  choices drive reach.
